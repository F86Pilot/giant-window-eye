    Zones

    Talk zones: the line plays once someone's feet are inside (or they walked through while it was being made). Put one around the speaker. No-photo zones: no photo is taken while their feet are inside, e.g. the far edges where people are too small for a good joke. No talk zones = talk anywhere.

      Voice settings

      Each line sends a small cropped photo of the person to Claude (Anthropic) to write the joke, and the line's text to ElevenLabs to speak it (a free voice on the Mac takes over if ElevenLabs fails). After the daily limit it uses free canned lines. Set both quiet hours the same to never be quiet. Keep this page open with the screen on; phones stop web audio when the screen locks.
