    Ignored Talk No-photo Recording

      What each area does

      Ignored: anything detected here isn't a person (by the middle of its box), for the eye, the voice and recordings. For mailboxes, bushes, shadows.
      Talk: the line plays once someone's feet are inside (or they walked through while it was being made). Put one by the yard speaker. None = talk anywhere.
      No-photo: the voice waits to take its photo while their feet are inside, e.g. far edges where people are too small for a good joke.
      Recording: a clip is only saved if their feet entered one (e.g. leave out the road). None = record every visit.
      The white dot on the picture is where the camera thinks the person's feet are.

      Changes take effect right away and are saved.

      Fast riders (bikes, scooters)
      Fast riders: when to talk

      At the talk zone: the line waits for them to reach a talk zone (up to 3 seconds), so it plays as they pass the speaker. If they already went through one, it plays right away.

      Sound when someone's noticed

      A short cackle or "Ooooh..." on the yard speaker as soon as their photo is taken (only for people who walked in, not ones first seen standing still, so parked cars never get one), while their line is picked. Never holds up the line: if it's still going, the line cuts it off.

      Skip things that don't move

      Only talk once their feet have moved a little, so bushes and mailboxes never get a line.

      "Still here?" line after (seconds)

      If someone is still around this long after their line, the eye writes a new one about what they're doing now (no recorded lines). 0 = off.

      ...then another every (seconds)

      Keeps going this often while they stay and move about; only for people who stay near where they were first spoken to (not walkers passing through); stops if they stand perfectly still for 20 seconds or leave. 0 = just the one.

      Most "still here" lines per person

      Each one costs about a cent.

      Skip anyone faster than (heights/second)

      Speed in the person's own heights per second: walking is about 0.6–1, jogging 1.5–2.5, bikes 3 or more. Faster than this = no line (and no cost). Each line shows its speed. 0 = off.
